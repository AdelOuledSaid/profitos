# ProfitOS V1.7.0 — Constraint Resolution Engine

V1.7.0 extends AI CFO Planner when no tested plan satisfies the user's financing ceiling.

The planner now proposes up to three explainable resolution strategies without inventing future revenue:

- increase available financing to the calculated requirement;
- reduce the discretionary initial decision amount by the calculated constraint gap;
- share the gap between additional financing and a lower initial decision amount.

The existing autonomous planner, reserve target, financing ceiling, deadline, debt-free analysis, Top 3 plans and AI CFO explanation remain in place.
